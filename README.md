# Welcome to GitHub Desktop!
Our game is about a teenager that goes by the name of june. June came from job interview to job interview, often getting rejected as he has a lack of experience in certain fields. In desperate need of money to pay for his mothers' hospitals due at the end of the month, he sees a flyer under his door, an arcade is hiring for a manager job. Without a doubt, he quickly signs up for the job and towards the end of his shift, he has to turn off all machines and turn off every light. He turned off everything...except this one arcade game that no matter how he tried, it doesn't turn off at all. Staring it it... finding a specific switch for the game to turn off, he gets sucked into the game which he has to play to survive. In the game, he has 5 lives. Using those lives, whenever he dies, he transforms into a ghost. He can jump over obstacles using his ghost that are too high up for him to reach normally in order to reach the next level and survive the game. His ghost allows him to bounce back and recover from his mistake rather than his mistakes punishing him, it allows him to progress.
